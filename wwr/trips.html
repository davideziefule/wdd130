Family River Adventure

Enjoy a memorable day on the river with family and friends. This beginner-friendly experience focuses on teamwork, beautiful scenery, and learning the basics of rafting with guidance from an experienced river guide.

Duration: 2 hours

Difficulty: Beginner

Recommended age: 8 and above

River Explorer Expedition

Discover the beauty of the river on an adventurous half-day journey. Paddle through flowing waters, enjoy the surrounding scenery, and work together as a team. This trip is ideal for visitors who want a little more excitement and an opportunity to develop their rafting skills.

Duration: 4 hours

Difficulty: Intermediate

Recommended age: 12 and above

Extreme Rapids Challenge

Take on a more demanding river adventure designed for thrill seekers with previous rafting experience. Navigate challenging rapids, coordinate your paddling, and enjoy an exciting outdoor experience under the supervision of qualified guides.

Duration: 6 hours

Difficulty: Advanced

Recommended age: 16 and above
